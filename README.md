# Field Training Management System

A web-based system designed to manage and organize the field training process for university students.

## Project Overview

The Field Training Management System provides an electronic platform that connects students, training companies, academic supervisors, and administrators. The system manages the field training workflow from submitting a training request to completing reports and following up on the student's progress.

## Main Features

* Student registration and login.
* Browse available training companies.
* Submit training requests.
* Companies can accept or reject student requests.
* Academic supervisors can review and approve training requests.
* Assign academic supervisors to students.
* Submit periodic training reports.
* Submit the final training report.
* Supervisors can review and evaluate student reports.
* Track student training progress.
* Notifications for important training updates.
* Separate dashboards for students, companies, supervisors, and administrators.

## Training Workflow

1. The student browses available training companies.
2. The student submits a training request.
3. The training company reviews the request and accepts or rejects it.
4. The academic supervisor reviews the accepted request.
5. The student starts the field training.
6. The student submits periodic training reports.
7. The academic supervisor reviews and evaluates the reports.
8. The student submits the final report and completes the training process.

## Project Purpose

The system aims to reduce manual procedures, organize field training data, improve communication between students and supervisors, and provide an efficient electronic solution for managing university field training.
